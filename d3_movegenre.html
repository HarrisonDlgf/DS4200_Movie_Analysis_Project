Movie Genre Trends Over Time

TMDB Dataset Analysis: Film Genre Popularity from 1990 to 2017

About This Visualization

This interactive chart displays the number of movies released per genre each year, based on the TMDB 5000 Movie Dataset. Click on genre names in the legend to show lines. Hover over the chart to see exact values.

Data source: TMDB (The Movie Database) via Kaggle

Takeaways

This chart shows how different movie genres have risen and fallen in popularity from 1990 to 2016. The green line at the top (likely Action or Adventure) dominated throughout most of this period, peaking around 2007 with over 90 movies released that year. Most genres saw steady growth through the 1990s and 2000s, reaching their highest points between 2007-2011, which represents a golden age of movie production. After 2011, almost every genre shows a sharp decline, with all lines dropping dramatically by 2016. This suggests that the traditional movie industry faced major changes in the 2010s, possibly due to the rise of streaming services, changing viewer habits, or shifts in how studios release films.
